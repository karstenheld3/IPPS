# STA Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using STA`.

Evidence sources: final document, planning notes (principle sentence, case source), conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### STA-PD-01: Principle Defined Before Writing

- Action: Agent stated the single principle in one sentence before drafting the unit
- Evidence: One TELL sentence per unit; no unit teaches two principles
- Failure indicator: Multiple principles in one unit, or no quotable principle sentence
- References: STA-ST-02, STA-CT-02

### STA-PD-02: Case Selected and Sourced

- Action: Agent selected a real or labeled composite case that demonstrates the principle
- Evidence: SHOW is attributed or labeled; numbers have context
- Failure indicator: Invented case presented as real, or case without numbers
- References: STA-EV-01, STA-EV-03

### STA-PD-03: Inductive Order Kept

- Action: Agent placed SHOW before TELL and kept SHOW lean
- Evidence: Case appears first, in 3-5 sentences without atmospheric details
- Failure indicator: Principle stated before the case, or case longer than 5 sentences with scenery
- References: STA-ST-01, STA-CT-01

### STA-PD-04: Steps and Quick Check Written

- Action: Agent wrote numbered single-action steps and an observable quick check
- Evidence: Numbered list with one verb per step; quick check sentence at unit end
- Failure indicator: Prose instructions, compound steps, or a vague check
- References: STA-ST-03, STA-ST-04, STA-EV-02, STA-CT-03

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### STA-QI-01: Recognition Strength

- Question: Will the target reader recognize their own situation in the SHOW case?
- Improvement tip: Match the case's company size, role, and tools to the declared audience; replace exotic cases with ordinary ones.

### STA-QI-02: Principle Memorability

- Question: Could a reader repeat the principle to a colleague a week later?
- Improvement tip: Shorten the TELL sentence to under 15 words and use a concrete contrast.

### STA-QI-03: Step Friction

- Question: Where will a reader most likely get stuck in the steps?
- Improvement tip: Add the expected result after that step, or split it into two steps.

### STA-QI-04: Chapter Arc

- Question: Does the chapter close return to the opening case and show the change?
- Improvement tip: End with one sentence describing what would have happened in the opening case if the steps had been followed.
