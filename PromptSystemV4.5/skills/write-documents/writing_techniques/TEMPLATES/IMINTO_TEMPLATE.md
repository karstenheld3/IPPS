## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Lede & Nut Graf** (hook & impact): 25-40 word active lede (who, what, when, where, why, how much) followed by reader impact and required action.
2. **Primary Fact Cluster** (core explanation): Most critical supporting evidence, cause, or mechanism, attributed to a named source or role.
3. **Secondary Fact Cluster** (remediation & open points): Next most important facts (e.g. fix and prevention) with conditional open points under active investigation.
4. **Background** (contextual): Least important historical context, related links, and references (deletable without loss of actionability).
