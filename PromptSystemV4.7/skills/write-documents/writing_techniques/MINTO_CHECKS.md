# MINTO Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using MINTO`.

Evidence sources: final document, planning notes (governing thought draft, key line draft), source data files, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### MINTO-PD-01: Governing Thought Drafted Before Prose

- Action: Agent wrote the governing thought as one sentence before writing sections
- Evidence: Planning notes or first draft show the governing thought; opening paragraph ends with it
- Failure indicator: Answer appears only in the closing section, or the opening contains no decision
- References: MINTO-AS-02, MINTO-AS-05, MINTO-AQ-04

### MINTO-PD-02: Key Line Built and Tested for Overlap

- Action: Agent listed 3-5 supporting claims and checked them for overlap and coverage before writing
- Evidence: Section headings are claims; no two headings restate the same point
- Failure indicator: More than 5 sections, topic headings, or two sections that make the same argument
- References: MINTO-TI-05, MINTO-AS-03, MINTO-AS-06, MINTO-ME-01

### MINTO-PD-03: Evidence Assigned per Claim

- Action: Agent mapped source evidence to each key-line claim before writing
- Evidence: Each section contains at least one sourced fact; unsupported claims are labeled as assumptions or removed
- Failure indicator: A section consists only of assertions or adjectives
- References: MINTO-TI-02, MINTO-TI-04, MINTO-DS-04

### MINTO-PD-04: Alternatives Considered

- Action: Agent identified the strongest alternative to the recommendation and addressed it
- Evidence: At least one alternative is named with the reason it loses
- Failure indicator: The source material mentions an alternative that the document omits
- References: MINTO-DS-01, MINTO-DS-02, MINTO-DS-03

### MINTO-PD-05: Closing Contains Action and Change Conditions

- Action: Agent ended with the requested action and the conditions that would change the recommendation
- Evidence: Final section names action, deadline or owner, and at least one change condition
- Failure indicator: Closing is a summary or a courtesy sentence
- References: MINTO-CL-01, MINTO-CL-03, MINTO-CL-04

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### MINTO-QI-01: Opening Sufficiency

- Question: If the decision-maker reads only the opening paragraph, can they decide?
- Improvement tip: Move the price, deadline, and main risk into the Answer sentence; cut Situation sentences that the reader already knows.

### MINTO-QI-02: Key-Line Order

- Question: Does the order of the key-line sections match how the reader will evaluate the decision (usually cost, benefit, risk)?
- Improvement tip: Reorder sections by the reader's first objection; put the section that answers it first.

### MINTO-QI-03: Evidence Strength

- Question: Is the strongest evidence in each section the first thing the reader sees?
- Improvement tip: Lead each section with measured data from the reader's own organization; move vendor or industry figures behind it.

### MINTO-QI-04: Honest Uncertainty

- Question: Does the document sound more certain than the evidence allows?
- Improvement tip: Add the range or confidence for forecasts ("payback 9-12 months") and state which assumption drives the range.
