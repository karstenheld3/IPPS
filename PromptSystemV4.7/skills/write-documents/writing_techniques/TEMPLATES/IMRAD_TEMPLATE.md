## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Abstract** (executive overview): ~100-word neutral summary covering core question, methods, quantified results, and primary limitation.
2. **Introduction** (context & research questions): Problem context, evidence gap, numbered research questions (RQ1..RQN), and explicit scope boundaries.
3. **Methods** (reproducibility): Data source, sample size, measurement procedure, statistical analysis, and exclusions with counts.
4. **Results** (empirical findings): Objective findings partitioned by research question with metrics, uncertainty ranges, and confidence levels (no interpretation).
5. **Discussion** (interpretation & limits): Findings interpretation, competing alternative explanations, and documented methodology limitations.
6. **Recommendations** (actionable translation): Concrete actionable recommendations directly derived from research question results.
