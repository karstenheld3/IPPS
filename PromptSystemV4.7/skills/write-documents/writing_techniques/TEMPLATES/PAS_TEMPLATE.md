## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Hook & Call to Action** (framing): One-sentence value proposition with reader outcome number and initial call-to-action button.
2. **Problem** (recognition): Reader's problem stated in customer words from reviews, tickets, or interviews.
3. **Agitation** (cost of inaction): Realistic cost of the status quo with at least one truthful quantified metric (time, cost, risk).
4. **Solution** (mechanism & benefits): Explains how mechanism solves the problem, followed by 2-4 concrete benefits.
5. **Proof** (credibility): Quantified empirical results with baseline, sample, and source, or attributed testimonials.
6. **Negative Boundary & Final CTA** (qualification): Honest boundary naming who the solution is not for, ending with confirmed call to action.
