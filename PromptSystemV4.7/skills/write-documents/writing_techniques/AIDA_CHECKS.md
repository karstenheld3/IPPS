# AIDA Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using AIDA`.

Evidence sources: final page or email, customer-language source notes (reviews, tickets, interviews), proof data, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### AIDA-PD-01: Product-Aware Fit and Section Order

- Action: Agent verified the reader is product-aware and followed Attention, Interest, Desire, Action order
- Evidence: Section order matches AIDA; attention hook leads, followed by interest mechanism and desire benefits
- Failure indicator: Non-AIDA order, pricing or feature list first, or attention hook placed at the bottom
- References: AIDA-ST-01

### AIDA-PD-02: Customer Language Collected

- Action: Agent used customer phrases from reviews, tickets, or interviews for the attention and interest statements
- Evidence: Attention and interest sections use concrete situations, not internal product vocabulary
- Failure indicator: Category described only with internal company jargon
- References: AIDA-ST-03, AIDA-CT-02

### AIDA-PD-03: Proof Verified

- Action: Agent checked every proof number and testimonial against source data
- Evidence: Proof contains baseline, result, sample size, and source; testimonials are attributed
- Failure indicator: Unquantified claims, missing baseline, or anonymous praise
- References: AIDA-EV-01, AIDA-EV-03

### AIDA-PD-04: Honest Limit and Single CTA

- Action: Agent wrote an honest limit and reduced the page to one call to action
- Evidence: One limit paragraph; one action verb and destination
- Failure indicator: No limit, or several competing actions
- References: AIDA-EV-02, AIDA-ST-02

### AIDA-PD-05: Hype and Passive Voice Removed

- Action: Agent removed hype adjectives, fake urgency, and passive claims in a final pass
- Evidence: No superlatives without evidence; urgency statements are true; claim sentences name an actor
- Failure indicator: Words such as revolutionary, seamless, world-class; invented deadlines
- References: AIDA-CT-01, AIDA-CT-03, AIDA-CT-04

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### AIDA-QI-01: Five-Second Test

- Question: Can a prospect state what the product does and for whom after five seconds on the page?
- Improvement tip: Put the outcome and the target user into the headline and the first sentence below it.

### AIDA-QI-02: Interest and Desire Progression

- Question: Does the transition from mechanism (Interest) to benefits (Desire) clearly explain what changes for the reader?
- Improvement tip: Pair each mechanism feature with one concrete daily benefit rather than abstract capability claims.

### AIDA-QI-03: Proof Relevance

- Question: Does the proof come from customers similar to the target reader?
- Improvement tip: Lead with the proof from the closest match in size and industry; move broader statistics below it.

### AIDA-QI-04: CTA Friction

- Question: What does the reader fear will happen after clicking?
- Improvement tip: Add one line under the button that removes the main fear ("No credit card. Cancel in two clicks.") if it is true.
