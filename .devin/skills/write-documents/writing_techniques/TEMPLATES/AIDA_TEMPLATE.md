## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Attention Hook & CTA** (framing): One-sentence value proposition with reader outcome number and initial call-to-action button.
2. **Attention Lead** (contrast): Distinct angle, unexpected operational contrast, or counter-intuitive insight capturing interest.
3. **Interest** (mechanism): Operating mechanism explained with concrete operational details (no hype) showing how the outcome works.
4. **Desire** (benefits): Features translated into 2-4 tangible reader benefits with quantified impact on time, cost, or risk.
5. **Proof** (validation): Quantified results with baseline, sample, and source; real attributed testimonials.
6. **Action & Qualification** (close): Negative boundary specifying who this is not for, followed by final call to action.
