## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Anecdote** (narrative hook): Concrete 2-5 paragraph narrative opening with one real person or organization, verified details, ending at point of tension.
2. **Nut Graf** (trend widening): Widening from specific case to systemic trend with at least one quantified metric and source; explains why the case is representative.
3. **Body Claim 1** (primary evidence): First thematic proof alternating evidence types (data, expert or document findings, secondary cases).
4. **Body Claim 2** (deepening evidence): Second thematic proof advancing the thesis, including a brief return to the anecdote subject.
5. **Counterpoint** (boundary & limits): Evidence for where the trend breaks down, limits of the argument, or opposing viewpoints.
6. **Kicker** (resolution): Closing return to the opening anecdote subject or planted detail showing what changed or remains open (no new arguments).
