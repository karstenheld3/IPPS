# IMINTO Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using IMINTO`.

Evidence sources: final document, fact list or source notes, incident or release records, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### IMINTO-PD-01: Facts Ranked Before Writing

- Action: Agent listed available facts and ranked them by importance to the reader
- Evidence: Paragraph order follows reader importance; background appears last
- Failure indicator: Chronological order of events, or reader action buried after background
- References: IMINTO-ST-03

### IMINTO-PD-02: Lede Written and Measured

- Action: Agent wrote the lede first and kept it within 25-40 words in active voice
- Evidence: First 1-2 sentences contain actor, action, result, time, and scale
- Failure indicator: Lede longer than 40 words, passive, or without numbers
- References: IMINTO-ST-01, IMINTO-CT-02

### IMINTO-PD-03: Nut Graf Placed Early

- Action: Agent stated reader impact and scope within 2 paragraphs after the lede
- Evidence: Paragraph 2 or 3 tells the reader what it means for them
- Failure indicator: Impact first appears in the second half of the document
- References: IMINTO-ST-02

### IMINTO-PD-04: Cut Test Applied

- Action: Agent verified that deleting the last paragraph removes no information the reader needs to act
- Evidence: Final paragraph contains context only; no summary paragraph exists
- Failure indicator: Closing paragraph repeats the lede or contains an action step
- References: IMINTO-ST-03, IMINTO-ST-04

### IMINTO-PD-05: Facts Verified and Labeled

- Action: Agent checked times, counts, and attributions against source records and labeled open points
- Evidence: Exact timestamps with timezone, named sources, explicit "not yet confirmed" labels
- Failure indicator: Vague times ("yesterday"), unattributed claims, or certainty about open investigations
- References: IMINTO-EV-01, IMINTO-EV-02, IMINTO-EV-03

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### IMINTO-QI-01: Lede Sufficiency

- Question: Can a reader who stops after the lede act correctly?
- Improvement tip: Add the single reader action or the all-clear status to the lede if it fits within 40 words; otherwise put it first in the nut graf.

### IMINTO-QI-02: Headline Precision

- Question: Does the headline tell the news, or only the topic?
- Improvement tip: Include the result and the scale ("47 minutes", "3,180 payments") in the headline.

### IMINTO-QI-03: Paragraph Density

- Question: Does each body paragraph carry one fact cluster that is less important than the previous one?
- Improvement tip: Split paragraphs that mix two importance levels; move the less important half further down.

### IMINTO-QI-04: Scannability for Web Delivery

- Question: Can a reader scanning on a phone find impact, action, and status within 10 seconds?
- Improvement tip: Add short informative subheadings after the nut graf and bold nothing except a single required action, if any.
