# PAS Example 01: WebsiteLandingPage

Website landing page written `using PAS as CONVERSION-COPYWRITER` (PAS variant). The product, customers, and all figures are fictional.

## Context

A scheduling app for restaurants runs search ads for "restaurant staff schedule template". Visitors are restaurant managers who know their scheduling problem but not the product. They decide within seconds whether to start a trial.

## Document

```markdown
# Build Next Week's Restaurant Schedule in 15 Minutes, Not 4 Hours

ShiftBoard creates staff schedules for restaurants from your team's saved availability and fills open shifts by text message.

[Start your 14-day trial]

## Sunday evening, spreadsheet open, three people unavailable

You know the routine. The schedule for next week is due, two servers changed their availability in a group chat, and one cook can only work mornings now. You rebuild the spreadsheet, send it out, and by Tuesday someone calls in sick anyway.

## What that spreadsheet really costs

Managers in our trial spent 4.1 hours a week on scheduling and shift swaps. At a manager cost of 28 EUR per hour, that is 4.1 hours x 52 weeks x 28 EUR = about 6,000 EUR a year, per location, spent on copying names between cells.

## How ShiftBoard handles it

Your staff enter their availability once in the app; ShiftBoard builds the weekly schedule around it and your labor budget.

- When someone calls in sick, the three available colleagues with the right role get a text within one minute; the first to accept gets the shift
- Shift swaps happen between staff in the app; you approve with one tap instead of rewriting the plan
- Before you publish, ShiftBoard shows the week's labor cost against your budget

## What trial restaurants measured

Across 120 restaurants in the 2026-Q2 trial, weekly scheduling time fell from 4.1 to 0.3 hours, and open shifts were filled in a median of 22 minutes.

"We stopped scheduling on Sunday evenings." - Operations lead, three-location bakery chain, customer since 2025

## Who ShiftBoard is not for

ShiftBoard is built for teams of 10-80 hourly staff. If you schedule hospital or care shifts with union rules and certification checks, a dedicated workforce management system will serve you better.

[Start your 14-day trial]

No credit card needed. Cancel in two clicks.
```

## Key Decisions

- PAS variant: search visitors know the problem ("staff schedule template") but not the product (PAS-ST-01)
- Headline states the outcome with numbers; the first sentence names product, mechanism, and audience (PAS-ST-03)
- Problem written in the manager's own situation (Sunday evening, group chat) rather than feature vocabulary (PAS-CT-02)
- Agitation uses one truthful cost calculation, no countdown or invented scarcity (PAS-CT-03)
- Proof with baseline, sample size, period, and an attributed testimonial (PAS-EV-01, PAS-EV-03)
- Honest limit as a technique element ("Who ShiftBoard is not for"), so the persona's The Catch trait is not used a second time (PAS-EV-02)
- One call to action, repeated once at the end, with a friction-removing line that is true (PAS-ST-02)
- Persona `CONVERSION-COPYWRITER` (Formality 2, Warmth 7): direct "you" voice; one persona trait, Inline Sanity Math, in the cost section
