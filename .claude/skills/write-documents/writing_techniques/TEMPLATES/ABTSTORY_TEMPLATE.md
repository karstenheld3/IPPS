## Section Inventory

Ordered list of sections for drafting and final prose:

1. **Opening ABT** (narrative tension): Context (AND), conflict with concrete metric/fact (BUT), and governing consequence (THEREFORE).
2. **Consequence 1** (primary proof): First consequence developed as a claim with evidence, optional secondary ABT unit, and conditional case passage.
3. **Consequence 2** (deepening & rival analysis): Second consequence developed with evidence and conditional competing explanations.
4. **Closing Resolution** (takeaway): Returns to opening BUT conflict, resolving tension and stating reader action or insight.
