# KABOB Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using KABOB`.

Evidence sources: final document, interview or source notes, data sources, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### KABOB-PD-01: Representative Case Selected

- Action: Agent selected an anecdote subject and checked that it represents the trend
- Evidence: Document states why the case is typical; source notes support the details
- Failure indicator: Case chosen for drama only, or no representativeness statement
- References: KABOB-EV-01, KABOB-CT-01

### KABOB-PD-02: Nut Graf Drafted Early

- Action: Agent drafted the nut graf before writing the anecdote and placed it within the first 5 paragraphs
- Evidence: Nut graf states the topic and contains one number
- Failure indicator: Topic first named after paragraph 5, or nut graf without a number
- References: KABOB-ST-02, KABOB-CT-02

### KABOB-PD-03: Body Evidence Mixed and Sourced

- Action: Agent alternated evidence types and attributed every claim
- Evidence: Body contains at least three evidence types (data, expert or document, second case, analysis) with sources
- Failure indicator: Uniform statistics block, or unattributed "experts say" claims
- References: KABOB-CT-03, KABOB-EV-02, KABOB-ST-04

### KABOB-PD-04: Counterpoint Researched

- Action: Agent looked for evidence that limits or contradicts the trend and included it
- Evidence: At least one paragraph states where the trend does not hold
- Failure indicator: Source material contains a limit that the document omits
- References: KABOB-EV-03

### KABOB-PD-05: Kicker Planned from the Anecdote

- Action: Agent chose a kicker detail from the anecdote during planning and returned to it at the end
- Evidence: Final paragraph references the opening subject or detail without a new argument
- Failure indicator: Kicker summarizes, forecasts, or introduces a new topic
- References: KABOB-ST-03, KABOB-CT-04, KABOB-ST-01

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### KABOB-QI-01: Anecdote Tension

- Question: Does the anecdote end at a moment of open tension that the nut graf then explains?
- Improvement tip: Cut the anecdote at the decision point; move the outcome to the kicker.

### KABOB-QI-02: Thread Continuity

- Question: Does the reader remember the anecdote subject by the time the kicker arrives?
- Improvement tip: Return to the subject once in the middle of the body with one new fact about their situation.

### KABOB-QI-03: Evidence Rhythm

- Question: Does any stretch of the body feel like a report rather than a feature?
- Improvement tip: Break statistic blocks with a short expert explanation or a second 2-3 sentence case.

### KABOB-QI-04: Kicker Payoff

- Question: Does the kicker give the opening detail a new meaning?
- Improvement tip: Show the same object or situation after the change the article describes, not a moral statement.
