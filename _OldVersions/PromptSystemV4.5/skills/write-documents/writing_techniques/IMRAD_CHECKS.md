# IMRAD Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using IMRAD`.

Evidence sources: final report, analysis notes or scripts, data exports, research question list, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### IMRAD-PD-01: Scope and Research Questions Fixed First

- Action: Agent wrote scope and numbered research questions before analyzing or writing results
- Evidence: Introduction ends with numbered questions; Results use the same numbering
- Failure indicator: Results contain questions not stated in the Introduction, or questions are missing results
- References: IMRAD-ST-03, IMRAD-ST-01

### IMRAD-PD-02: Method Documented for Reproduction

- Action: Agent recorded data source, sample, period, tools, analysis steps, and exclusions
- Evidence: Methods section contains all six elements with numbers
- Failure indicator: "We analyzed the data" without source, period, or exclusions
- References: IMRAD-EV-03

### IMRAD-PD-03: Complete Findings Reported

- Action: Agent compared the list of all findings against the report and kept null and contradictory results
- Evidence: Every research question has a result, including "no measurable difference" results
- Failure indicator: A research question disappears from Results or Discussion
- References: IMRAD-CT-01, IMRAD-EV-01

### IMRAD-PD-04: Results Separated from Interpretation

- Action: Agent kept interpretation out of Results and named all surviving hypotheses in the Discussion
- Evidence: Results contain numbers and labels only; Discussion lists alternative explanations and limitations
- Failure indicator: Causal language in Results, or a single explanation where the data allow several
- References: IMRAD-CT-02, IMRAD-EV-02, IMRAD-ST-04

### IMRAD-PD-05: Abstract Written Last

- Action: Agent wrote the abstract after all other sections and kept it neutral at about 100 words
- Evidence: Abstract matches the final results and states the main limitation
- Failure indicator: Abstract contains claims not in Results, promotional words, or more than 150 words
- References: IMRAD-ST-02, IMRAD-CT-03

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### IMRAD-QI-01: Reader Reuse

- Question: Could another team reuse the data and method for their own question?
- Improvement tip: Add the exact data export filters and a link to the analysis script or query.

### IMRAD-QI-02: Effect Size Meaning

- Question: Does the reader understand what the effect sizes mean in practice?
- Improvement tip: Translate the main effect into a concrete unit the audience uses (hours per week, euros per month) in the Discussion.

### IMRAD-QI-03: Confidence Calibration

- Question: Do confidence labels match sample size, period, and design?
- Improvement tip: Lower the label by one level when the sample covers one season, one site, or fewer than 100 units, and state why.

### IMRAD-QI-04: Recommendation Proportionality

- Question: Do the recommendations claim more than the results support?
- Improvement tip: Prefer "extend the pilot" or "test X" over company-wide decisions when results come from a single pilot.
