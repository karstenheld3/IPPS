# ABTSTORY Rules

Output standards for documents written `using ABTSTORY` (And, But, Therefore plus Story Spine for embedded cases).

**Writing quality:** Apply `APAPALAN_RULES.md` and `MECT_WRITING_RULES.md`. Key rules: AP-PR-07 (be specific), MW-WC-10 (explicit over figurative), MW-VO-01 (active voice).

## Rule Index

Structure (ST)
- ABTSTORY-ST-01: Opening follows AND, BUT, THEREFORE order
- ABTSTORY-ST-02: Each ABT unit has exactly one BUT
- ABTSTORY-ST-03: Story Spine is used only for embedded cases
- ABTSTORY-ST-04: Closing links back to the opening BUT

Content (CT)
- ABTSTORY-CT-01: Opening contains no AND-AND-AND fact list
- ABTSTORY-CT-02: THEREFORE states a concrete consequence, finding, or action
- ABTSTORY-CT-03: AND contains only context the reader accepts

Evidence (EV)
- ABTSTORY-EV-01: The BUT is backed by a concrete fact or number
- ABTSTORY-EV-02: Competing explanations that survive the evidence are named
- ABTSTORY-EV-03: Embedded cases are real and attributed, or labeled as composite

## ABTSTORY-ST-01: AND, BUT, THEREFORE Order

**BAD:**
```
Therefore, cities should rethink tree planting. Trees cool streets. But many new trees die.
```

**GOOD:**
```
Street trees cool neighborhoods by up to 4 degrees Celsius in summer. But in the fictional
city of Riverton, 38% of newly planted trees died within three years. Therefore, the city
now budgets for watering, not only for planting.
```

## ABTSTORY-ST-02: One BUT per Unit

**BAD:** "But trees die, but budgets are cut, but residents complain about leaves."

**GOOD:** One BUT per paragraph-level unit; other problems move to later sections.

## ABTSTORY-ST-03: Story Spine for Embedded Cases Only

**BAD:** The whole policy essay is written as "Once upon a time... Until finally...".

**GOOD:** One 5-sentence case inside section 2 follows the Story Spine; the rest of the essay is argument.

## ABTSTORY-ST-04: Closing Links to the BUT

**BAD:** Closing introduces a new topic (air quality) never mentioned before.

**GOOD:** "The question was never whether trees cool cities; it was whether they survive long enough to do it."

## ABTSTORY-CT-01: No AND-AND-AND Opening

**BAD:** "Trees provide shade, and they absorb rain, and they increase property values, and they reduce noise."

**GOOD:** "Trees provide shade and absorb rain. But a third of new trees die before they grow large enough to do either."

## ABTSTORY-CT-02: Concrete THEREFORE

**BAD:** "Therefore, we need to think differently."

**GOOD:** "Therefore, Riverton moved 22% of its planting budget to three years of watering contracts."

## ABTSTORY-CT-03: AND Is Accepted Context

**BAD:** AND introduces a disputed claim the reader has never heard.

**GOOD:** AND states a fact the audience already accepts ("Trees cool streets in summer").

## ABTSTORY-EV-01: BUT Backed by a Fact

**BAD:** "But things are more complicated than they seem."

**GOOD:** "But 38% of new trees died within three years (municipal survey of 4,200 plantings, 2022-2025)."

## ABTSTORY-EV-02: Surviving Explanations Named

**BAD:** "Trees die because nobody waters them." (soil compaction is equally supported by the data)

**GOOD:** "Missed watering explains most losses; compacted soil explains the rest, and the survey cannot separate the two in 14% of cases."

## ABTSTORY-EV-03: Real or Labeled Cases

**BAD:** An invented gardener with a name and quotes presented as a real person.

**GOOD:** "A composite case based on three crew interviews: ..." or a named, sourced real case.
