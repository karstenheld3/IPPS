# ABTSTORY Checks

Audits process discipline and quality improvement for documents written `using ABTSTORY`.

Evidence sources: final document, planning notes (logline draft), source data, conversation history.

## Process Discipline (PD)

### ABTSTORY-PD-01: Logline Drafted First

- Action: Agent drafted a one-sentence AND-BUT-THEREFORE logline before writing the opening
- Evidence: Opening paragraph follows the logline order with one BUT
- Failure indicator: Opening starts with the conclusion or lists facts without a conflict
- References: ABTSTORY-ST-01, ABTSTORY-ST-02, ABTSTORY-CT-01

### ABTSTORY-PD-02: Conflict Selected from Evidence

- Action: Agent selected the BUT from the strongest evidence and attached the supporting fact
- Evidence: BUT sentence contains a number or concrete fact with a source
- Failure indicator: BUT is vague ("things are complicated") or unsupported
- References: ABTSTORY-EV-01

### ABTSTORY-PD-03: Alternatives Preserved

- Action: Agent checked whether other explanations survive the evidence and named them
- Evidence: Body names competing explanations where the source supports them
- Failure indicator: Source lists several causes; the document presents one as the only cause
- References: ABTSTORY-EV-02

### ABTSTORY-PD-04: Story Spine Scoped

- Action: Agent limited Story Spine to embedded cases and labeled composite cases
- Evidence: Story Spine sequence appears only inside case passages; cases are attributed or labeled
- Failure indicator: Entire document is a fable, or invented people appear as real
- References: ABTSTORY-ST-03, ABTSTORY-EV-03

### ABTSTORY-PD-05: Closing Connected

- Action: Agent ended by linking back to the opening BUT with a concrete THEREFORE
- Evidence: Final paragraph references the original conflict and states the consequence or action
- Failure indicator: Closing introduces a new topic or ends with a generic statement
- References: ABTSTORY-ST-04, ABTSTORY-CT-02

## Quality Improvement (QI)

### ABTSTORY-QI-01: Tension Strength

- Question: Would a reader who knows the AND be surprised by the BUT?
- Improvement tip: Replace a mild BUT with the most counter-intuitive finding the evidence supports, and state its size.

### ABTSTORY-QI-02: Logline Clarity

- Question: Can the whole document be summarized in one ABT sentence?
- Improvement tip: Write that sentence at the top of your notes; cut sections that do not serve its THEREFORE.

### ABTSTORY-QI-03: Momentum in Long Texts

- Question: Does the text lose energy after the opening?
- Improvement tip: Restart each major section with a smaller ABT unit that connects to the main THEREFORE.

### ABTSTORY-QI-04: Case Economy

- Question: Do embedded cases illustrate the argument or take it over?
- Improvement tip: Limit each case to 4-6 sentences and end it with one sentence that returns to the argument.
