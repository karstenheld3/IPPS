# AIDA Rules

Output standards for documents written `using AIDA` (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action, plus proof, honest limit, and one call to action).

**Writing quality:** Apply `APAPALAN_RULES.md` and `MECT_WRITING_RULES.md`. Key rules: APAPL-PR-07 (be specific), MECT-VO-01 (active voice), MECT-VO-02 (address reader as "you"), MECT-WC-02 (plain language).

## Rule Index

Structure (ST)
- AIDA-ST-01: Sections follow Attention, Interest, Desire, Action order
- AIDA-ST-02: Page contains exactly one call to action
- AIDA-ST-03: Headline states the reader's outcome

Content (CT)
- AIDA-CT-01: No hype adjectives
- AIDA-CT-02: Features are translated into reader benefits
- AIDA-CT-03: Interest and desire claims stay truthful; no fake urgency
- AIDA-CT-04: Active voice in every sentence that makes a claim

Evidence (EV)
- AIDA-EV-01: Proof is quantified with a baseline and source
- AIDA-EV-02: An honest limit is stated
- AIDA-EV-03: Testimonials are real and attributed

## AIDA-ST-01: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action Order

**BAD:** Pricing table or feature matrix first, attention hook at the bottom.

**GOOD:** Headline (attention), mechanism details (interest), benefits and proof (desire), honest limit, call to action.

## AIDA-ST-02: One Call to Action

**BAD:** "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "Download the whitepaper", and "Join our newsletter" on one page.

**GOOD:** "Start your 14-day trial" as the only action, repeated once at the end of the page.

## AIDA-ST-03: Outcome Headline

**BAD:** `ShiftBoard: The Next-Generation Scheduling Platform`

**GOOD:** `Build Next Week's Restaurant Schedule in 15 Minutes, Not 4 Hours`

## AIDA-CT-01: No Hype Adjectives

**BAD:** "Our revolutionary, world-class, seamless platform empowers teams."

**GOOD:** "ShiftBoard fills open shifts from your staff's saved availability."

## AIDA-CT-02: Benefit Translation

**BAD:** "Availability sync API with push notifications."

**GOOD:** "When a cook calls in sick, the three available colleagues get a message within one minute."

## AIDA-CT-03: Truthful Claims and Urgency

**BAD:** "Only 3 spots left! Revolutionary breakthrough ends tonight!" (no real limit exists)

**GOOD:** "Every week without automation costs a manager about 4 hours of manual scheduling."

## AIDA-CT-04: Active Voice

**BAD:** "Schedules are created automatically and shifts are filled."

**GOOD:** "ShiftBoard creates the schedule and fills open shifts."

## AIDA-EV-01: Quantified Proof with Baseline

**BAD:** "Customers save tons of time."

**GOOD:** "Across 120 trial restaurants, scheduling time fell from 4.1 to 0.3 hours per week (internal trial data, 2026-Q2)."

## AIDA-EV-02: Honest Limit

**BAD:** The page claims the product fits every business.

**GOOD:** "ShiftBoard is built for teams of 10-80 hourly staff. If you schedule hospital shifts with union rules, choose a dedicated workforce system."

## AIDA-EV-03: Real Testimonials

**BAD:** "'Best tool ever!' - Happy Customer"

**GOOD:** "'We stopped scheduling on Sunday evenings.' - Operations lead, three-location bakery chain (customer since 2025)"
